5 Disks on 3 towers:
There are 5 locations on each tower that the disks will be grabbed from but due to constraints in the problem there are a limited number of possible locations for each disk. The lowest value disk (1) is the biggest and at the bottom of the tower, this correlates to Position 1: P1 on Tower 1: T1 or shorthand as T1P1, then disk 2 starts at T1P2, disk 3 starts at T1P3, disk 4 starts at T1P4 and disk 5 starts at T1P5

Disk 5 will always be grabbed from the bottom of the tower and should be easy since its z is fixed. (3 total positions)
Disk 4 can be either placed on the bottom of the tower or on top of disk 5 so it has variation in where it is. (6 total positions)
Disk 3 can be found at P1, P2, and P3 but will never go higher than that. (9 total positions)
Disk 4 can be located at P1, P2, P3, P4 but will not be found at P5 on any tower (12 total positions)
Disk 5 can be located anywhere on the towers and could possibly be placed at P1, P2, P3, P4, P5
